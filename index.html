How a building becomes a product

One system. Six repeatable steps. Every time.

This is the entire MMY US process, from an empty lot to a finished, occupied home.

01 / Site

Site & permits

Confirm your lot, your local code, and your zoning requirements.

02 / Configure

Configure

Choose your model, building size and facade from a pre-engineered catalog.

03 / Manufacture

Manufacture

85% of the work happens off-site — structure, MEP, fixtures and finishes, built as complete modules.

04 / Transport

Transport

Modules ship by truck or rail, nationwide, from a single facility.

05 / Assemble

Assemble

We set, connect and seal the modules on-site, while ground works run in parallel — not in sequence.

06 / Handover

Handover

A permanent, code-compliant building, signed off and ready to occupy.

Modular construction builds the majority of a structure in a factory setting, then transports finished modules to the site for assembly — instead of building everything on-site from scratch. The controlled environment leads to tighter tolerances, fewer delays, and more consistent quality.

The MMY US system cuts carbon emissions by roughly 35% compared with traditional construction — an average reduction of about 32 tons of CO₂ per unit.

A single-family home takes about 8 weeks from approval to completion, versus a national average near 48 weeks for traditional construction — roughly 5x faster, thanks to our cold-formed steel system.

Repeating a standardized design removes the extra architectural and engineering costs of a one-off build. On orders above 100 units, developers typically see 15–35% lower development costs through volume.

Yes. MMY US units meet the same local building codes and regulations as traditionally built housing — the difference is in the construction process, not the compliance or quality standards.
